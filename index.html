SDG 2: Zero Hunger

SDG 2 is a global initiative that seeks to address the pressing issue of hunger and malnutrition worldwide. It recognizes that access to sufficient, safe, and nutritious food is a fundamental human right and a prerequisite for sustainable development. The goal emphasizes the importance of ensuring that all people, especially vulnerable populations, have access to adequate food throughout the year.

Source: United Nations Sustainable Development Goals

Cause and Effects of Hunger

Hunger is a complex issue with multiple interconnected causes and effects. Poverty, conflict, climate change, and poor infrastructure are some of the key factors contributing to food insecurity. The effects of hunger are equally devastating, leading to malnutrition, stunted growth in children, and increased susceptibility to diseases.
